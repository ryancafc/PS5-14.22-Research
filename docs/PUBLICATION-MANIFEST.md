## What is deliberately not published

- The **eight original Windows-local Phase 10 deliverables** (including its full evidence register, exact final dossier, README replacement and project manifest) were not available to this publication workflow.
- The **49 original Windows-local Phase 1–9 deliverables** were not uploaded in full.
- Original downloaded Sony WebKit source archives, extracted trees and copyrighted source files.
- Approximately **336 MB** full comparison CSV and the original compact audit CSVs.
- Unpublished local audit scripts, complete original per-file hashes and raw processing logs.
- Retail firmware PUP files, device secrets, executable exploit payloads or other materials requiring separate assessment.

The project's reported computation and integrity checks are documented, but the GitHub publication by itself is **not a complete independent reproduction package**.

## How to obtain a verbatim publication later

If the original Windows-local Phase 10 files are supplied as attachments or uploaded by an authorised maintainer, they may be reviewed and substituted for the synthesized reports in a subsequent change. Review citations, licensing, disclosure requirements and hash manifests before replacing anything. The original project claims must not be backfilled with invented file hashes or unverifiable source links.

## Research and publication verdict

The local Phase 9 audit reportedly returned **PASS WITH QUALIFICATIONS** and Phase 10 **PUBLICATION READY WITH QUALIFICATIONS**. This GitHub publication preserves those caveats. It does **not** demonstrate a working PS5 14.22 jailbreak or a complete named security-fix inventory.

If any source, claim, or correction is disputed, open a review issue and provide the original primary evidence and exact relevant revision.
